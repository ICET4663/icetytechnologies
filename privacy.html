Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Icety Technologies respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and your rights, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you send us, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Project information you share when requesting a quote or working with us.
  • Billing records such as invoices and payment confirmations.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotes.
  • To deliver, support and invoice our services.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Payments

Online payments are processed by our payment provider. We do not see or store your full card details.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run our business (such as payment, hosting and email providers) or when required by law.

Client data in projects

Where we build or maintain systems that hold your customers' data, we process that data only on your instructions and apply security measures such as access control, encryption in transit and secure authentication.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us.
