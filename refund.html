Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how refunds work for services purchased from Icety Technologies.

Before work begins

If you cancel a project before any work has started, your deposit will be refunded in full.

After work begins

If a project is cancelled after work has started, we will refund the part of your payment that exceeds the value of work already completed, based on the milestones in your quote. We will share a summary of completed work with you.

Completed and delivered work

Payments for milestones that have been delivered and approved are not refundable. If delivered work does not match the agreed scope, we will first fix it at no extra cost. If we cannot fix it, you may request a partial or full refund for that milestone.

Maintenance plans

Monthly maintenance plans can be cancelled at any time and will not renew for the next month. The current month already paid for is not refundable.

Third-party costs

Fees paid on your behalf to third parties, such as domains, hosting or app store accounts, are not refundable by us.

How to request a refund

Email us with your name, invoice or payment reference and the reason for your request. We respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method or bank account within 5–10 business days.
